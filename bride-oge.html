Her DearLover story

Mine actually started with a giveaway! I won a DearLover giveaway during the CEO's birthday, and honestly, I don't think I fully realised at the time just how much of my wedding experience they would become a part of. I'm someone who knows what she wants, but I also knew my budget, so I wanted to make sure every choice counted. We worked on my traditional wedding, white wedding and after-party looks, and each one had to give something different — I didn't want three versions of the same bride.

What did you want your wedding wardrobe to make you feel?
I wanted to feel expensive! But seriously, I wanted every look to make me feel confident, beautiful and like the best version of myself. I knew what I wanted, and I also knew I didn't want to just spend money for the sake of spending money. From traditional bride to white wedding bride to after-party babe, I wanted each version of me to have her own moment.

What was your favourite look?
How do you expect me to choose? Every look was excellent in its own way. My after-party look was definitely one of my favourites because I could relax, have fun and just enjoy being the bride — it felt playful and free, which is very me. Although, ask me tomorrow and I might say my traditional look. That's how much I loved them all.

What did DearLover mean to your wedding experience?
DearLover made me feel like my looks actually mattered — and I don't just mean "wear this and stand there." There was intention behind every look. Winning the giveaway was such a beautiful surprise, but the experience itself ended up being even more special than I expected. I came in knowing what I wanted, knowing my budget, and wanting everything to be excellent — and DearLover understood the assignment. It was fun, intentional and honestly one of the most exciting parts of getting married.
